About Serenity

Hi, I'm Serenity — professional tattoo artist at Red Dagger Tattoo in Pasadena, Texas.

I specialize mostly in illustrative blackwork that includes heavy stippling, detailed linework, botanicals, birds, and creatures that don't quite exist. But I am more than happy to do full color when the idea calls for it.

You usually won't see your design until the day of your appointment, and that's by design. I budget in time to make adjustments and I'd rather you tell me that it isn't right than let you walk out with something you'll live with forever and only like half of.

Got an idea? Let's talk about it!
