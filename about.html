About Serenity

Hi, I'm Serenity! I'm a professional tattoo artist at Red Dagger Tattoo in Pasadena, Texas, working with clients from across the greater Houston area.

I specialize in Blackwork, Neotraditional, and Illustrative tattoos. I'm especially drawn to large-scale pieces, flash designs, retro themes, and full creative freedom, though I'm more than happy to do full color when the idea calls for it.
